Seitans Realm

Seitans Realm is a vegan restaurant that serves a variety of dishes made with seitan, a meat substitute made from wheat gluten. The restaurant has a cozy and welcoming atmosphere, and the staff are friendly and knowledgeable about the menu. Some of the standout dishes include the seitan wings, the seitan burger, and the seitan stir-fry. The restaurant also offers a selection of vegan desserts, including cakes and cookies. Overall, Seitans Realm is a great option for anyone looking for delicious vegan food in Columbus.

Soul 2 Go

Soul 2 Go is a trendy coffee shop that offers a wide range of specialty drinks and pastries. The shop has a modern and inviting atmosphere, making it a popular spot for students and professionals. They are known for their high-quality coffee and creative menu items. Overall, Soul 2 Go is a great place to enjoy a delicious drink and a tasty treat.

Stuffedenuff

Stuffedenuff is a unique bookstore that specializes in rare and out-of-print books. The store has a warm and eclectic atmosphere, with shelves filled with treasures from various genres. The staff are knowledgeable and passionate about literature, making it a great place for book lovers to discover new finds. Overall, Stuffedenuff is a must-visit for anyone interested in exploring the world of rare books.

For the Love of Dough

For the Love of Dough is a charming bakery that offers a variety of freshly baked goods. The shop has a cozy and welcoming atmosphere, with the aroma of fresh bread filling the air. They are known for their artisanal breads, pastries, and custom cakes. Overall, For the Love of Dough is a great place to satisfy your sweet tooth and enjoy delicious baked goods.
